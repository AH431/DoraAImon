INMA BORRELLA: In this segment, we will introduce a framework
for supply chain visibility.
The framework may be useful to map out supply chain visibility
requirements for implementing or evaluating a supply chain
visibility initiative.
This framework is inspired by one proposed
by Somapa and colleagues.
You can find the reference to the paper on the slide.
And the framework breaks down supply chain visibility
into three key areas--
access to information, quality of information,
and utilization of information.
Access to information refers to the ability
to capture and transfer information.
Quality of information refers to the ability
to ensure a certain quality level for the data gathered
in terms of timeliness, accuracy, and completeness,
as well as the processing of that information
to generate actionable insights through data visualization
and analytics.
Utilization of information refers
to the ability to use the information to create value.
Namely, the information can serve
to improve operational performance
and to create new supply chain capabilities.
